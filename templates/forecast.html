Simulation methodology & limitations

The source xAPI-Edu-Data.csv provides the original activity total for each record. A reproducible seeded multinomial allocation splits that exact total among eight hypothetical weeks. The weekly timing and pattern are made up for teaching purposes; they cannot be recovered from the real dataset.

The previous-week, three-week average and four-week linear-trend methods are backtested on these generated weeks. No class labels are used to create synthetic activity, and the synthetic forecast is not used to predict learners' L/M/H performance.

For this selected group, {{ simulation.source_total }} raw activities were distributed across the hypothetical weeks. Their total is preserved exactly: {{ simulation.allocation_total }} allocated activities. This verifies arithmetic conservation, not the assumed weekly schedule.
