# AI Solution — School Learner Performance Predictor and Study Tutor

The School Learner Performance Predictor and Study Tutor is a Python-based Artificial Intelligence prototype designed for the **school education sector**, using the publicly available xAPI-Edu-Data learning-management benchmark. By analysing subject, grade, school stage, learning-resource visits, raised-hand and discussion participation, announcement activity and recorded absence categories, the machine-learning model estimates Low, Middle or High learner performance classifications. A teacher-facing dashboard highlights patterns for human review, while a retrieval-based NLP study assistant answers curated school-level questions in subjects such as mathematics, science, English and homework revision. The goal is to support proactive teaching and personalised academic guidance without replacing teacher judgement. This solution fits the assignment theme **“An AI Solution for Industries”** by applying Python AI techniques to educational analytics and school learning support. It is a demonstration using anonymous public benchmark records, not a deployed school system.
