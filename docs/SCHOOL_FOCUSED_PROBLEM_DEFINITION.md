# Problem Definition — School Education

Schools need practical ways to recognise patterns that may indicate a learner requires additional academic support. Teachers may have multiple classes and limited time to review learner engagement across lessons, school resources, participation, absences and subject performance. Without timely and meaningful insights, some learners may miss early opportunities for extra revision, support with attendance barriers, or help from teachers and parents or guardians. Learners may also struggle to find reliable explanations when completing schoolwork outside classroom hours.

The proposed **School Learner Performance Predictor and Study Tutor** explores whether Artificial Intelligence can help organise school learning data into actionable, understandable information. A supervised machine-learning model classifies anonymous records from the xAPI-Edu-Data benchmark into Low, Middle or High academic-performance groups using classroom participation and online learning activity. Its dashboard presents observed school-stage, subject and grade-level patterns, while a curated NLP chatbot provides introductory academic explanations and study techniques. Teachers remain responsible for reviewing circumstances before recommending any support.

The solution aligns with **“An AI Solution for Industries”** by applying AI to a problem in the school education industry. Better academic support has potential benefits for learners, families and communities, although this proof of concept **does not demonstrate improved outcomes, predict dropout, or establish effectiveness in any actual school**.
